## 📚 Core Vocabulary (20 Words)

### 1. **Forehand** /ˈfɔːrhænd/ (n.)
- **Definition:** A shot in tennis made with the palm of the hand facing the player, hitting the ball on the dominant side
- **Example:** "The forehand is usually the first stroke beginners learn in tennis."
- **中文:** 正手（网球术语）

### 2. **Backhand** /ˈbækhænd/ (n.)
- **Definition:** A shot in tennis made with the back of the hand facing the player, hitting the ball across the body
- **Example:** "His backhand has improved significantly with practice."
- **中文:** 反手（网球术语）

### 3. **Serve** /sɜːrv/ (n.)
- **Definition:** The stroke that starts each point in tennis
- **Example:** "She has a powerful serve that scores many aces."
- **中文:** 发球（网球术语）

### 4. **Grip** /ɡrɪp/ (n.)
- **Definition:** The manner in which a player holds the racket
- **Example:** "The continental grip is recommended for beginners."
- **中文:** 握拍方式

### 5. **Stance** /stæns/ (n.)
- **Definition:** The position of the feet and body when preparing to hit the ball
- **Example:** "Keep a wide stance for better balance."
- **中文:** 站姿

### 6. **Swing** /swɪŋ/ (n.)
- **Definition:** The motion of hitting the ball
- **Example:** "Practice your swing until it becomes natural."
- **中文:** 挥拍动作

### 7. **Racket** /ˈrækɪt/ (n.)
- **Definition:** The bat used to hit the ball in tennis
- **Example:** "Choose a racket that feels comfortable in your hand."
- **中文:** 球拍

### 8. **Court** /kɔːrt/ (n.)
- **Definition:** The rectangular area where tennis is played
- **Example:** "The clay court plays slower than hard courts."
- **中文:** 球场

### 9. **Baseline** /ˈbeɪslaɪn/ (n.)
- **Definition:** The line at the end of the court
- **Example:** "Stay behind the baseline when returning serve."
- **中文:** 底线

### 10. **Net** /net/ (n.)
- **Definition:** The dividing line in the middle of the court
- **Example:** "The ball hit the net and didn't go over."
- **中文:** 球网

### 11. **Score** /skɔːr/ (n.)
- **Definition:** The number of points a player or team has
- **Example:** "The score is 15-30 in his favor."
- **中文:** 比分

### 12. **Advantage** /ədˈvæntɪdʒ/ (n.)
- **Definition:** A point scored after deuce that gives a player the lead
- **Example:** "He had advantage but lost the next point."
- **中文:** 局分

### 13. **Deuce** /djuːs/ (n.)
- **Definition:** A tied score of 40-40 in tennis
- **Example:** "They reached deuce for the third time."
- **中文:** 平分（40-40）

### 14. **Ace** /eɪs/ (n.)
- **Definition:** A serve that the opponent cannot return
- **Example:** "She served 10 aces in the match."
- **中文:** 发球得分

### 15. **Volley** /ˈvɒli/ (n.)
- **Definition:** A shot hit before the ball bounces
- **Example:** "He approached the net for a volley."
- **中文:** 截击

### 16. **Lob** /lɒb/ (n.)
- **Definition:** A high shot intended to go over the opponent
- **Example:** "She hit a perfect lob to win the point."
- **中文:** 挑高球

### 17. **Slice** /slaɪs/ (n.)
- **Definition:** A shot with backspin that stays low
- **Example:** "The slice is effective on grass courts."
- **中文:** 削球

### 18. **Topspin** /ˈtɒpspɪn/ (n.)
- **Definition:** Forward rotation that makes the ball dip down
- **Example:** "Add topspin to keep the ball in play."
- **中文:** 上旋

### 19. **Follow through** /ˈfɑloʊ θruː/ (phrasal verb)
- **Definition:** Continue the motion after hitting the ball
- **Example:** "Always follow through on your strokes for better control."
- **中文:** 随挥动作

### 20. **Rally** /ˈræli/ (n.)
- **Definition:** A sequence of shots between players
- **Example:** "They had an impressive 20-shot rally."
- **中文:** 连续对打

## 🎯 Authentic Expressions (15)

### 1. **"Game, set, match"**
- **Meaning:** The game is completely finished
- **Usage:**宣布比赛完全结束
- **Example:** "Game, set, match! He won in straight sets."

### 2. **"Love"** (in tennis scoring)
- **Meaning:** Zero points
- **Usage:**网球计分中的零分
- **Example:** "The score is 15-love."

### 3. **"Break point"**
- **Meaning:** One point away from winning the opponent's serve game
- **Usage:**破发点
- **Example:** "She saved three break points in that game."

### 4. **"Fault"**
- **Meaning:** An invalid serve
- **Usage:**发球失误
- **Example:** "That was a foot fault on the serve."

### 5. **"Let"**
- **Meaning:** The point is replayed (usually due to interference)
- **Usage:**重赛
- **Example:** "It's a let because of the noise from the crowd."

### 6. **"Out"**
- **Meaning:** The ball landed outside the lines
- **Usage:**出界
- **Example:** "That shot was clearly out."

### 7. **"In"**
- **Meaning:** The ball landed inside the lines
- **Usage:**界内
- **Example:** "The ball was called in."

### 8. **"New balls please"**
- **Meaning:** Requesting new tennis balls (usually after 7-9 games)
- **Usage:**请求换新球
- **Example:** "New balls please, umpire!"

### 9. **"Fault on my racket"**
- **Meaning:** Acknowledging one's own mistake
- **Usage:**承认是自己的失误
- **Example:** "That hit the frame. Fault on my racket."

### 10. **"Good rally!"**
- **Meaning:** Praising a long exchange of shots
- **Usage:**称赞精彩的对打
- **Example:** "Good rally! That was impressive."

### 11. **"Nice shot!"**
- **Meaning:** Complimenting a well-executed stroke
- **Usage:**好球
- **Example:** "Nice shot! Perfect placement."

### 12. **"Break serve"**
- **Meaning:** Win the game when opponent is serving
- **Usage:**破发
- **Example:** "He managed to break serve at 4-3."

### 13. **"Hold serve"**
- **Meaning:** Win the game when serving
- **Usage:**保发
- **Example:** "She held serve to level the match."

### 14. **"At the net"**
- **Meaning:** Playing near the net
- **Usage:**上网
- **Example:** "He's very comfortable at the net."

### 15. **"From the baseline"**
- **Meaning:** Playing from the back of the court
- **Usage:**底线
- **Example:** "She stayed back and hit groundstrokes from the baseline."

## 💡 Learning Tips

1. **Start with grip**: Learn the continental grip as it's most versatile
2. **Focus on form first**: Don't worry about power initially
3. **Practice consistently**: Short daily practice beats long weekly sessions
4. **Watch the ball**: Track the ball from your opponent to your racket
5. **Stay relaxed**: Tension affects your swing negatively
6. **Use your non-dominant hand**: Try playing with your weak hand too
7. **Play with better players**: Challenge yourself to improve faster
8. **Record yourself**: Video your strokes to analyze your form
